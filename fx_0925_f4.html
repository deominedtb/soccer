A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Türkiye are promoted and new to League A: B, C, B in the three previous editions, and this is their first League A match since the competition began in 2018. In 2024–25 they were second in League B Group B4, W3 D2 L1, 9–6, behind Wales, then beat Hungary 3–1 at RAMS Park and 3–0 in Budapest in the A/B play-off (6–1 on aggregate). In World Cup qualifying they were second in Group E behind Spain, W4 D1 L1, 17–12, the defeat 0–6 to Spain in Konya. They then won the play-off path 1–0 against Romania in Istanbul and 1–0 in Kosovo. At the World Cup, their first since 2002, they lost 0–2 to Australia and 0–1 to Paraguay, which put them out, then beat the United States 3–2 (Wikipedia, Daily Sabah). France have been in League A in every edition. In 2024–25 they won Group A2, W4 D1 L1, 12–6, beat Croatia on penalties in the quarter-final (0–2, 2–0), lost the Finals semi-final 4–5 to Spain and beat Germany 2–0 for third. They qualified for the World Cup unbeaten, W5 D1, 16–4, and finished fourth: 3–1 Senegal, 3–0 Iraq and 4–1 Norway in the group, then 3–0 Sweden, 1–0 Paraguay and 2–0 Morocco, 0–2 to Spain in the semi-final and 4–6 to England for third. How League A has treated promoted sides: the eight promoted into it for 2022–23 and 2024–25 took 0.79 points per match (W9 D11 L28), scored 0.92 and conceded 1.73 per match, and won 3.29 corners against 5.92. At home they took 0.96 points per match (W5 D8 L11). On matchday one they went W3 D1 L4, and W2 D1 L1 at home. None won its group; one finished second, two third and five fourth (ESPN standings). The structural headline is a promoted side missing its captain and its left-sided creator, against a new coach without his first-choice left-backs, two of his three most-used centre-backs and his holding midfielder. Head to head: six meetings, Türkiye W1 D1 L4, the last two in Euro 2020 qualifying (2–0 in Konya, June 2019; 1–1 at the Stade de France, October 2019) (Sports Mole, Daily Sabah).
