A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Northern Ireland are promoted: first in League C Group C3 in 2024–25 on 11 points, 11 scored and 3 conceded (W3 D2 L1, ahead of Bulgaria, Belarus and Luxembourg). They are back in League B after two editions in League C, having been relegated from it in 2020–21. Georgia stay in League B: third in Group B1 in 2024–25 on 7 points, 7–6 (W2 D1 L3, behind Czechia and Ukraine). They then won the B/C relegation play-off against Armenia, 3–0 in Yerevan and 6–1 in Tbilisi. In World Cup qualifying Georgia were third in Group E on 3 points, 7–15 (W1 L5): their only win was 3–0 against Bulgaria, and four of the five defeats came against Spain and Türkiye. Northern Ireland were third in Group A on 9 points, 7–6 (W3 L3, behind Germany and Slovakia), then lost 0–2 to Italy in the play-off semi-final. Neither side went to the World Cup. The structural headline is a promoted side with an unchanged coach and a young squad, away to a League B side whose recent competitive record was set mostly against Spain and Türkiye. The eight sides promoted from League C into League B for 2022–23 and 2024–25 took 1.13 points per match over 46 matches.
