B · Style & early-season reality

Lecce are a 4–3–3 with an attacking brief and a season of evidence against it. Twenty-eight goals in thirty-eight matches is 0.74 a game, and it was paired with fifty conceded; the eight draws and the final-day survival are what a team looks like when it cannot score and cannot reliably keep the ball out either. Di Francesco has been kept, so the method is not changing, but the personnel available to execute it has been thinned in exactly the department that was already the problem. Camarda, Sottil and Cheddira all went back to their parent clubs, Banda did not report, and the replacement is one €5m forward, Geubbels, who has not yet scored. The one genuine addition to the spine is Ivan Ilic on loan from Torino, a midfielder rather than a defender, in a squad that signed no centre-back and whose first-choice left-back has a facial injury a week into the season. The opening win over Venezia is a real result and the right kind of start; it is also a result against a side that was not in this division last season, and it does not tell us that 0.74 goals a game has become something else.

Roma are the opposite proposition and the more legible one. Thirty-one conceded in thirty-eight is the foundation Gasperini inherited and kept, and the summer was spent addressing the other end: a €35m centre-forward in Castro, a permanent deal for Malen, and Mora from Porto behind them, with Molina at right wing-back to feed the 3–4–2–1’s wide overloads and Koulierakis added to a back three that already had Mancini, Ndicka and Hermoso. Matchday one was the loudest possible statement of what that money bought — four goals, a Malen hat-trick, three Dybala assists — and it is also the single caution attached to reading it. One home match against Fiorentina, with the new centre-forward not among the scorers, is a small sample carrying a very large scoreline. The structural case for Roma here does not rest on that afternoon; it rests on 59 goals scored and 31 conceded across a full season, a manager retained, and a fixture record in which they have not lost a league meeting with Lecce in fourteen years and have won the last three without conceding. What the summer changed is that the attack is now expensive as well as effective; what it did not change is the defence, which was the reason for third place.
