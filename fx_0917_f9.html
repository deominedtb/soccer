A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Neither club is promoted. Plzeň finished 3rd in the 2025–26 Czech First League — 18-9-8, 60 scored, 38 conceded, 63 points — and reached this league phase for the third season running by beating Crvena zvezda 5–4 on aggregate after extra time. Union Saint-Gilloise topped the 2025–26 Belgian regular season and finished 2nd after the play-offs — 25-11-4 over 40 matches, 66 scored, 27 conceded — won the Belgian Cup against Anderlecht, then lost their Champions League qualifier to Bodø/Glimt 5–6 on aggregate. The structural headline is that Plzeň’s last season was coached by a man dismissed three weeks ago, and their league publishes no xG, while Union’s long, well-measured series meets a squad missing six players tonight.

B · Style & early-season reality

Plzeň were a possession side in the Czech league last season — 56.0% of the ball, 16.6 shots for and 11.0 against, 5.91 corners won against 3.71 — with a 40% clean-sheet rate and 0.54 goals conceded per first half. This season the defence has come apart: 1.88 goals conceded per league match, 0.88 per first half, BTTS in 75%, and a 5–5 at Teplice, with the corner balance narrowing (4.88/4.25). Under Kováč the four league matches have been tighter (four goals conceded), and the home leg against Crvena zvezda produced 6.89 xG, but that is one match. The centre of defence has been rebuilt in the last fortnight of the window (Vavro, Pališčák), and the attack is without Vydra; how the new back line fits is a CV and a few matches.

Union under Hubert are an organised, efficient side, extreme at home: 14-2-0 at home in the league after his appointment (31–7), 1.62 xG for and 1.02 against over 30 matches, 5.63 corners won and 3.50 conceded, and only 40% of matches over 2.5. Away from home the record was far weaker (4-7-3). This season’s six league matches are more prolific (3.17 goals and 2.12 xG per match, five of six over 2.5) in a 3-4-1-2. Tonight’s midfield is missing Van de Perre and Zorgane and the attack Florucz; Kričfaluši, Mofokeng and Aquino are the summer additions most likely to cover, and Aquino arrived on 2 September.

D · Early-season risk notes

  • Plzeň’s last season and most of this one belong to a dismissed coach. Kováč has five competitive matches; Hyský had led the side since October 2025.
  • Plzeň’s defence is newly assembled and their league publishes no xG. Vavro and Pališčák arrived on 1 and 3 September; the only Plzeň xG figure this season is one 120-minute qualifier.
  • Union are missing six players. Van de Perre, Zorgane and Florucz suspended, Mac Allister, Sykes and Rodríguez injured.
  • Union’s record splits sharply by venue. Under Hubert, 14-2-0 at home and 4-7-3 away in the league.
  • Most sensitive: 1X2, handicap, Plzeň team goals and discipline. Least sensitive: corners and halves, where both clubs’ series are complete.
