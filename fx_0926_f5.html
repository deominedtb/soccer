A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Iceland are relegated from League B: third in Group B4 in 2024–25 on 7 points, 10–13 (W2 D1 L3, behind Wales and Türkiye, ahead of Montenegro), then beaten 2–5 on aggregate by Kosovo in the B/C play-off (1–2 in Pristina on 20 March 2025, 1–3 in the listed home leg in Murcia, Spain, three days later). Their division history reads A/B/B/C, so this is their first League C campaign. In World Cup qualifying they were third in Group D on 7 points, 13–11 (W2 D1 L3, behind France and Ukraine), beating Azerbaijan 5–0 at home and 2–0 in Baku, drawing 2–2 at home to France, and losing 1–2 in Paris, 3–5 at home to Ukraine and 0–2 to Ukraine in Warsaw. Estonia stay in League C: third in Group C1 on 4 points, 3–9 (W1 D1 L4, behind Sweden and Slovakia, ahead of Azerbaijan), in their first League C campaign after promotion from League D; their history reads C/D/C/C.
