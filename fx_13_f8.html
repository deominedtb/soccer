A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Reduced-depth card: research was capped at two searches per club plus the referee lookup. The RESEARCH SPEC fields not pursued here are both clubs' 2025–26 final positions, goal columns, xG, shot volume, home/away splits and foul rates. Those absences are a budget decision, not a finding.

Neither club is promoted this season — Leeds came up in 2025–26 and this is their second consecutive Premier League campaign — and neither changed manager. Fabian Hürzeler, appointed in June 2024, signed an extension in May 2026 running to 2029; Daniel Farke continues at Leeds. What separates them is the scale of the summer. Brighton made ten senior signings and let nineteen players go; Leeds made five. And one of those deals runs directly between the two teams on the pitch: Pascal Struijk moved from Leeds to Brighton for a reported £20m, so a player sold by the visitors in this window may face them for the hosts. Two matchweeks in, Leeds have four points and Brighton three, from wildly different-looking matches: Brighton beat Aston Villa 4–0 and then lost a seven-goal game at Chelsea, while Leeds drew 1–1 at home with Brentford, another club on this slate. The venue record is emphatic and one-directional: Leeds have never won in ten visits to the Amex, though they won the reverse fixture at Elland Road 1–0 in May 2026 through Dominic Calvert-Lewin.

  • Brighton — ten in, nineteen out, and the club record broken on a centre-back. Hürzeler continues, so the system is stable even as the personnel are not. The record signing is Luka Vuskovic from Tottenham at a reported £46m rising to £50m — Tottenham also appear on this slate at f11, and the traffic ran both ways, with Jan Paul van Hecke moving to Spurs. The rest of the window is unusually well documented on fees: Zadok Yohanna from AIK Stockholm for £24m on a five-year deal, Pascal Struijk from Leeds for £20m, Chema Andrés from Stuttgart for £19m, Jaouen Hadjam from Young Boys for £11m, Svoboda via a €5m release clause at Venezia, and Matías Orozco from Deportivo Cali for £3.5m. Igor Julio left for Burnley at a reported £3m among the nineteen departures. Femi Azeez completed a move from Millwall days before this match and could feature.   • Leeds — five signings, but the two biggest are a goalkeeper and a centre-back. Farke continues from the promotion and survival seasons. The club record went on James Trafford, signed from Manchester City for a reported £40m plus £5m in add-ons — and Manchester City appear on this slate at f10. Tarik Muharemović, a 23-year-old Bosnian centre-back, arrived from Sassuolo at a reported £34m; Nico Elvedi came from Borussia Mönchengladbach, on this slate at f3, for a reported £7m; and Harry Wilson arrived on a free from Fulham, on this slate at f9. Leeds lose Joe Rodon for eight to ten weeks with what Farke called a serious hamstring injury, and Mateo Joseph remains out with a knee problem; Elvedi comes in for Rodon and makes his full Premier League debut.
