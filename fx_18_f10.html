Neither club is promoted. PSV are three-time defending champions, won the 2025–26 title (their 27th) and are second on 13 points from five. After a 2–2 at Fortuna Sittard they have won four straight: 2–1 Excelsior, 5–1 Groningen, 6–1 Utrecht and 3–1 at Ajax. That is 18 scored and 6 conceded, with no clean sheet in five. Midweek they drew 1–1 at home to Shakhtar in the Champions League, ending the winning run. Sparta are 11th on five points (one win, two draws, two defeats), last playing a 2–2 at home to Zwolle. The structural headline is that Sparta’s head coach and a large share of its senior core are new.
