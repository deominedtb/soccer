A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

One promoted side, and the promoted side is not the one whose identity has changed. Schalke 04 won the 2025–26 2. Bundesliga — first on 70 points, 21W–7D–6L, 50 scored and 31 conceded for +19, eight points clear of SV 07 Elversberg, with a home record of 13W–3D–1L (29–14) and an away one of 8W–4D–5L (21–17). They were relegated in 2022–23, five years after finishing second to Bayern, and return for a 55th top-flight season in the club's 123rd. The Bundesliga's treatment of promoted sides is the usual question, but Schalke arrive with an unusual version of it: the manager who won promotion, Miron Muslić, is unchanged, and the squad that won it is very largely unchanged too, because the club spent €2.65m in total this summer and carries a squad market value of €67.33m — the third-lowest in the division and €49.82m behind fifteenth-placed Hamburger SV on €117.15m. Union Berlin are not promoted; this is their eighth consecutive Bundesliga season and they finished 11th in 2025–26 on 39 points, 10W–9D–15L, 44 scored and 58 conceded for −14, split sharply by venue — 5W–7D–5L and 26–26 at home, 5W–2D–10L and 18–32 away. What is new at Union is everything above the players. They have had three head coaches in five months: Steffen Baumgart until 11 April 2026, Marie-Louise Eta as interim to the end of the campaign — the first woman to head-coach a Bundesliga club — and Mauro Lustrinelli appointed on 21 May 2026. And with him has come the single largest structural fact on this card: Union are playing a back four for the first time since their promotion in 2019, abandoning the back five that has defined every Bundesliga season the club has ever played.

  • Union Berlino — high turnover, concentrated almost entirely at centre-back, and a new manager on 21 May. Lustrinelli, 50, is Swiss and arrives from FC Thun, where he took a promoted side to the first Swiss Super League title in that club's history while scoring 80 goals; his stated aim at Union is "intensity, dynamism and energy going forward", which in system terms means a high press and possession from a back four. The squad work follows that. Out went both long-serving centre-backs: Diogo Leite released on 14 May after 136 competitive appearances and Danilho Doekhi to Lazio on 14 July after 137. In came three replacements — Marvin Friedrich free from Borussia Mönchengladbach on 10 June, Zeno Van Den Bosch free from Royal Antwerp on 15 June, and Stanley Nsoki permanently from Hoffenheim for €1.5m on 1 July, the only fee Union paid. Up front, Emmanuel Latte Lath arrived on loan from Atlanta United on 2 August, a 27-year-old Ivorian with 27 goals in 59 Championship appearances for Middlesbrough. Kastriot Imeri followed Lustrinelli from Thun. Also out: Alex Král released to FC Copenhagen on 16 July, Ilyas Ansah to Hull City, and goalkeeper Yannic Stein to Hansa Rostock on 27 May for an undisclosed fee. Weighted as the spec asks: three centre-backs in and two out, a centre-forward on loan, no defensive midfielder signed — Rani Khedira, 32, is the man asked to anchor the new press — and no senior goalkeeper brought in to replace Stein, with Frederik Rønnow the expected starter. That GK line is a gap in the ledger rather than a finding.
  • Schalke 04 — low turnover by design, the largest fee spent on a defensive midfielder, and no managerial change at all. Muslić, 43 and Austrian-Bosnian, stays, and works from a flat 4–3–3 or a 3–4–2–1 whose wing-backs drop into the back line — described in the season preview as "a tight, centralised form of defending" against stronger opposition. The window was a survival budget, not a promotion budget. In, with fees: Satoshi Tanaka from Fortuna Düsseldorf for €1m, a 23-year-old defensive midfielder and the club's biggest outlay of the summer; Junior Dina Ebimbe from Eintracht Frankfurt for €800k; Junior Adamu from SC Freiburg for €800k, a 25-year-old centre-forward; Kevin Müller from 1. FC Heidenheim for €50k, a 35-year-old goalkeeper; Maximilian Wöber free from Leeds United, a 28-year-old centre-back; and Robin Gosens on loan from Fiorentina at left-back. Edin Džeko had arrived from Fiorentina in January 2026. One source additionally reports Hwang Hee-chan on loan from Wolverhampton; it is recorded here as reported by a single source and not corroborated elsewhere. Out: Ilyes Hamache to SC Cambuur for €200k, Mauro Zalazar free to Kustošija, goalkeeper Luca Podlech on loan to Aalesund, plus a set of loans and demotions. The GK/CB/DM ledger is therefore genuinely addressed on this side — €1m at defensive midfield, a free centre-back, a €50k goalkeeper behind Loris Karius, 33, who is the established starter — but the totality is €2.65m, into a division whose fifteenth-placed club is valued at nearly €50m more. Squad of 30, average age 27.0, the second-oldest in the league.

B · Style & early-season reality

Union Berlin are, for the first time in their Bundesliga history, trying to be a different kind of team — and the evidence that they can be is two matches old and already compromised. The back five was not a preference at Union, it was the institution: it carried them from the 2019 promotion through eight seasons, and Lustrinelli has replaced it with a back four and a high press in his first summer. Everything in the window served that idea — two long-serving centre-backs moved on, three new ones brought in, a forward with Championship goals added on loan, Khedira at 32 asked to hold the base of a pressing shape. The problem is arithmetic: two of those three centre-backs are injured, so the defensive line executing an unfamiliar structure is not the line that was assembled to execute it. What the two matches have produced is consistent with a side mid-transition and nothing more precise than that — a 3–3 salvaged by late goals, then a 0–4 at Leverkusen, seven conceded in 180 minutes and more than anyone else in the division. Against that, last season's home column is the one number on this page suggesting the venue matters: 26 scored and 26 conceded at the Alte Försterei across 17 matches, against 18 and 32 away. Gap: no xG, possession, foul-per-game or corner series was obtainable for Union in either season, so the press is described here from its manager's stated intent and his record at Thun, not from measurement.

Schalke are the opposite proposition in every respect, and the more legible of the two. There has been no managerial change, no stylistic reinvention and almost no spending: Muslić won the second division by eight points with a flat 4–3–3 or a 3–4–2–1 that drops wing-backs into the line, and he is running the same thing in the Bundesliga with a squad reinforced by €2.65m. The early returns split exactly the way that profile predicts. Beaten 3–0 by Augsburg, they then held the champions to 0–0 at home — a result that says the centralised defensive block transfers to this level, and says nothing at all about whether the attack does. They have not scored a Bundesliga goal, from 15 attempts and 6 on target, with a €800k centre-forward and a 32-year-old loanee left-back among the summer's answers. Karius at 33 has been busy and effective, 7.5 saves a match at 83.3%. The discipline signature is already distinctive and it is the cleanest in the league: 11 fouls in two matches, fewest of any club, which is what a side defending in a narrow block without chasing possession tends to produce. The caution is that both of these two-match samples contain an extreme — a 0–3 and a 0–0 with Bayern — and a promoted side's third fixture against a mid-table club is a different test from either. Gap: no xG series for Schalke either, and no corner data for either club.

D · Early-season risk notes

  • Union's back four is three matches old and understaffed. The club has played a back five in every Bundesliga season since promotion in 2019; Lustrinelli changed it in his first summer, bought three centre-backs to do it, and two of them — Friedrich and Nsoki — are injured until mid-to-late September. Every defensive number on this page was produced either by a different system or by an incomplete version of the new one.
  • Union have had three head coaches in five months. Baumgart to 11 April, Eta as interim to the end of the season, Lustrinelli from 21 May. Last season's 44 scored and 58 conceded were produced under two of them and neither is here, which makes the 2025–26 columns a description of a club rather than of this team.
  • Schalke's promotion numbers were made in a division they beat by eight points. 70 points, 50 scored and 31 conceded came against 2. Bundesliga opposition, and the summer that followed was €2.65m — so the side now facing Bundesliga fixtures is very nearly the side that won the second tier, at the third-lowest squad value in the division and the second-oldest average age.
  • Both two-match samples contain one extreme and one uninformative result. Union's 0–4 at Leverkusen and Schalke's 0–0 with Bayern are the two matches doing most of the work in the goals, BTTS and totals reasoning above, and neither resembles the fixture being played tonight.
  • Most sensitive: the cards family, where the appointed official is unknown and the two clubs' foul rates are at opposite ends of the division — the one row on this card with a named structural question and no evidence at all to answer it. Least sensitive: the 1X2, where three live prices sit against two well-documented and genuinely opposite problems, neither of which the market has resolved.
