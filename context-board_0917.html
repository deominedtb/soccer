B · Style & early-season reality

Levski under Velázquez dominate the ball at home in Bulgaria: 62.5% possession, 11.6 shots for and 5.3 against, and 6.17 corners won against 2.61 conceded per league match last season, conceding 0.22 goals per first half. The summer’s effect is concentrated in midfield: Serginho and El Moubarik started at Vratsa, El Moubarik is out tonight, and Grujić arrived in mid-August.

Salzburg under Röhl have become a very high-volume, low-foul side in six league matches: 65.0% possession, 21.3 shots and 8.2 on target per match, 2.84 xG for and 1.14 against, 8.33 corners won, and only 5.0 fouls and 0.83 cards per match — against 9.7 fouls and 1.75 cards last season. The evidence for the rebuilt spine is six league matches and four qualifiers: Boma, Mazurek and Barry started at Ried, Tabaković leads the scoring, and neither Salzburg goalkeeper has a settled run; Lindstrøm’s arrival is a CV, not a match.
