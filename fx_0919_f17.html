D · Early-season risk notes

  • Three league matches each is the thinnest sample anywhere on this slate, and one of Stuttgart’s three is a 1–5 at Bayern München which alone carries much of their 2.67 conceded, 3.30 expected goals against, 19.67 shots faced and 7.33 corners conceded. It cannot be excluded and should not be treated as typical. Their single home match produced a 4–1 win.
  • The division has moved further than either club, and further than any division on this board. The Bundesliga is running at 3.96 goals per match and 82.1% over 2.5 against 3.24 and 63.7% across the whole of 2025–26 — a gap of 0.72 goals a game — on 28 matches. Every row above that leans on the 26/27 divisional line is leaning on about a fifth of a season, and the totals row leans on it heavily.
  • Neither club changed coach or made a signing large enough to explain a change of level, which cuts both ways. It means last season’s 34-match records are unusually applicable — Hoeneß is in his fourth season and Kovač his second full one — and it means Stuttgart’s defensive numbers have no structural explanation at all. A 1.73-goal rise in expected goals against with the same coach and no defensive sale is either three matches of noise or something no source reached has identified.
  • No injury or suspension list exists for either side in any source reached. On a card whose defensive rows are the entire story, not knowing which back line Stuttgart field is a material gap, and one club channel indicates Dortmund will make three changes to their starting eleven without the detail being retrievable.
  • Most sensitive: the Asian handicap and 1X2 — both need a margin from three matches per club, and Stuttgart’s three contain a four-goal defeat and a three-goal win. Least sensitive: total goals and team goals — the first because the divisional rate, both clubs’ 34-match rates, both clubs’ three-match rates and both xG lines all point the same way, the second because four scoring rates across two seasons sit between 2.00 and 2.67 with the expected goals agreeing and neither club overperforming them.
