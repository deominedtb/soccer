  • Cagliari’s league position is the most misleading number on this card. Fifth on 9 points, 4 scored and 2 conceded — from four matches in which they were outshot 19.00 to 12.25 per match, conceded 1.73 xG per match and 9.50 deep completions, and won three by exactly one goal. Their actual goal difference is +0.50 per match and their expected-goal difference is −0.53. Every rate on their side of the strip — the 50% clean-sheet rate, the 25% BTTS, the 25% over 2.5 — is downstream of that gap.
  • Udinese’s defence is not the defence that produced last season’s 48 goals conceded. Four defenders arrived this summer (Traoré, Vojvoda, Abankwah, Palma), a goalkeeper arrived and another left, and Kristensen was sold to Atalanta as part of a near-€50m outgoing. The 2.50 conceded and 2.49 xG against per match are four matches of a new back line, and the 25/26 figures beside them belong to a different one.

Serie A is running at 3.02 goals per match, 58.5% over 2.5 and 61.0% BTTS this season, against 2.43, 45.8% and 45.3% across the whole of 2025–26 — and at a 14.6% draw rate, the lowest of the five divisions on this board. That is 41 matches against 380. Both clubs’ own 25/26 rates sat close to the old divisional line, so where this card uses the new one it is using a sample a tenth the size.
