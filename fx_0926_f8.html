A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

North Macedonia are promoted from League C: winners of Group C4 in 2024–25 on 16 points, 10–1 (W5 D1 L0, ahead of Armenia, the Faroe Islands and Latvia), and this is their first League B edition (League C in 2020–21, 2022–23 and 2024–25 in ESPN’s standings; “the first time we are in this competition”, Darko Pančev to the FFM’s magazine). In World Cup qualifying they were third in Group J on 13 points, 13–10 (W3 D4 L1, behind Belgium and Wales), reached the play-offs through the Nations League and lost the semi-final 0–4 in Copenhagen. Switzerland are relegated from League A: fourth in Group A4 in 2024–25 on 2 points, 6–14 (W0 D2 L4, behind Spain, Denmark and Serbia), their first relegation to League B (SRF). They then won qualifying Group B on 14 points, 14–2 (W4 D2 L0, ahead of Kosovo), won World Cup Group B on 7 points (W2 D1 L0 against Qatar, Bosnia-Herzegovina and Canada), beat Algeria 2–0 and Colombia on penalties after 0–0, and went out to Argentina 1–3 after extra time, 1–1 at 90 minutes, with Embolo sent off in the 72nd minute. The structural headline is that both records split hard by the level of the opponent, and tonight each side meets a level it has not faced in this form: North Macedonia went W8 D2 L0 (20–2) against League C and D sides and W0 D3 L2 (3–13) against League A sides, and have not played a League B side; Switzerland went W5 D2 L0 (18–3) against League B sides and W0 D2 L4 (6–14) against their League A group, and arrive without their captain, their first-choice centre-forward and three other World Cup regulars.
