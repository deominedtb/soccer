Reduced-depth card. The PSG window and the Brest managerial context are carried from those two cards.

Neither club is promoted. PSG are the holders of the Champions League and the Super Cup, and have made their worst domestic start in fourteen years: 2–2 at Rennes, 2–2 at Lille, 1–2 at home to Monaco — two points, 5 scored, 6 conceded, 13th or 14th. In midweek they beat Slovan Bratislava 6–1 in the Champions League, with Ousmane Dembélé scoring twice. Brest are 7th and unbeaten on five points: 2–2 with Le Mans, 2–2 with Toulouse, then 2–1 at Le Havre, with midfielder Kamory Doumbia scoring twice there and now Ligue 1’s top scorer on four. The structural headline: all six of the league matches between these two sides this season — three each — produced at least three goals.

D · Early-season risk notes

  • PSG’s winless start came with a forward line three players removed from last season. Barcola, Ramos and Kolo Muani out, Torres, Akliouche and Godts in: the attacking combinations are new, and the 6–1 came against Slovan, not a Ligue 1 side.
  • Brest’s back line is not the one that took five unbeaten points. Chardonnet is out and Lala doubtful, so the defence that conceded five in three is further weakened.
  • Three matches each, with PSG on a Wednesday-to-Sunday turnaround. Rotation after a Champions League night was not established.
