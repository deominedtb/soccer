# ANALYSIS_PROMPT — outside research before Phase 5

Paste the block below into Gemini (Deep Research) or Perplexity, attach
context-board_N.html (or paste its text), and fill in the brackets.

Timing: run it 2–3 hours before kickoff. Send the output back with
"Phase 5" about an hour before kickoff, once lineups are out.

Send back: "Phase 5", the tool's full output, and any thoughts of your own.

```
You are doing pre-match research to check a statistical context card that
another analyst built. The card is attached. It already contains every team
statistic (goals, xG, BTTS, cards, fouls, corners, home/away splits, league
averages, the referee's record). Do NOT re-collect or restate statistics.
Your job is what the card cannot know: what happened in the last 7 days.

Slate: [DATE], fixtures [f1, f2, ... or "all on the card"].

RULES
- Do not use betting tips, prediction sites, tipsters or odds of any kind.
- Only facts from the last 7 days, unless a longer-term fact is still
  current (a long-term injury, a points deduction). Every fact gets a
  source link and its publication date.
- Mark each fact CONFIRMED (club or league statement, manager quote),
  REPORTED (journalist / local press) or RUMOUR. Never present a rumour
  as fact.
- If you cannot find something, write "not found". Do not fill gaps
  from memory: lower-league squads change heavily every summer, and
  anything you remember from earlier seasons is likely out of date.
- For lower-division clubs, search the club's official site, the
  manager's pre-match press conference, local newspapers and BBC Sport
  local pages, FotMob and Flashscore team pages, and Transfermarkt for
  loans.

FOR EACH FIXTURE, use this exact shape, headed with the card's ID
(e.g. "f3 · Home – Away"):

1. Absences: injured, suspended, doubtful, for each side. Name, position,
   status, expected return, source and date. Check suspensions from
   yellow-card accumulation and recent red cards.
2. Returns and arrivals: players back from injury, new loans or signings
   since the card's sources, loans recalled.
3. Predicted lineup: formation and XI for each side if a credible source
   predicts one; say which source. Note any rotation risk (a cup game
   within 3 days either side).
4. Context: manager change or pressure, ownership or financial problems,
   points deduction, motivation (relegation, play-off race), pitch,
   weather, travel distance. Facts only.
5. Contradictions with the card: anything you found that disagrees with
   a fact on the card, quoting the card's line and your source.
6. My read: up to 3 markets you think this news supports. Only use
   families the card rates "Well-suited" or "OK" (or the readable side
   of a "Split"). Name the exact line (e.g. "Bromley corners over 4.5",
   "BTTS yes", "Under 2.5 goals", "Home +1"). For each one, one sentence
   on which news item and which card figure it rests on. No prices.

End with one short list: the single most important piece of news on the
slate and which fixture it affects.
```
