B · Style & early-season reality

Barcelona are producing the most extreme attacking numbers on this board and they are not a small-sample illusion — every column has moved in the same direction from an already-dominant baseline. Expected goals 4.31 per match against 2.62 last season; non-penalty xG 3.69; shots 21.00 against 18.21; shots on target 8.50 against 6.76; deep completions 15.33 against 11.53. Possession is unchanged at 68.48% against 68.84% and the press is unchanged at a PPDA of 7.01 against 7.09 — so this is the same system doing more damage inside it, with Gordon and Adeyemi in place of Lewandowski, Ferran Torres and Rashford. The defensive side has drifted slightly the other way: 1.00 conceded per match against 0.95, and expected goals against 1.17 against 1.33. The corner column is the clearest single measure of territorial control on this card: 8.17 won and 2.83 conceded, against a divisional 4.94, and against Racing Santander three days ago they won 18 corners to nil. The one figure that should temper all of this is the opposition: three of their six league opponents are the clubs carded on this board at 14:00, 16:15 and 18:30, two of whom sit 11th and 10th.

Sevilla are a pressing, foul-heavy side that has improved defensively and stopped creating. Expected goals 1.11 per match, and non-penalty expected goals just 0.86 — so roughly a quarter of what they generate comes from the spot — against 1.04 and 0.92 last season. The defensive improvement is the real change and it is substantial: expected goals against down from 1.61 to 1.16, goals conceded from 1.58 to 1.00, and a clean-sheet rate up from 15.8% to 33.3%, on a back line that lost Nianzou and Juanlu Sánchez and gained Juan Iglesias free and Vlachodimos on loan in goal. They press hard and are pressed hard: PPDA 8.90 with opponents at 7.49, both among the most aggressive pairs on this board, and possession has fallen from 50.98% to 43.83%. The cost is the discipline column, where they lead the board: 3.50 cards per match against 2.89 last season, on 15.17 fouls against a divisional 12.36. Their first-half attacking record is the most extreme on their side of the card — one first-half goal in six matches, 0.17 per match, against a divisional 1.43.
