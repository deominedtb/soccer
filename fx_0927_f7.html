A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Israel are the relegated side; Ireland stayed in League B. Israel were fourth in League A Group A2 in 2024–25 on 4 points, 5–13 (W1 D1 L4, behind France and Italy on 13 and Belgium on 4). The win was 1–0 against Belgium in Budapest, and the draw 0–0 at the Stade de France. They had come up by winning League B Group B2 in 2022–23. In World Cup qualifying they were third in Group I (W4 L4, 19–20, behind Norway and Italy): they beat Estonia and Moldova home and away and lost all four to Norway and Italy, 6–17. Ireland were third in League B Group B2 in 2024–25 on 6 points, 3–12 (W2 L4: both wins over Finland; 0–2 twice against Greece, 0–2 and 0–5 against England). They then kept their place by beating Bulgaria 2–1 in Plovdiv and 2–1 in Dublin (March 2025). In qualifying they were second in Group F (W3 D1 L2, 9–7), reaching the play-offs with a 2–0 home win over Portugal and a 3–2 win in Budapest, then went out in Prague: 2–2, and 3–4 on penalties (26 March 2026). Format: group winners are promoted to League A. Runners-up go into promotion play-offs and fourth-placed sides into relegation play-offs against League C runners-up (Wikipedia’s League B page, citing UEFA). How the division treats relegated sides: the eight sides relegated from League A for 2022–23 and 2024–25 went W9 D4 L2 at home against sides that had stayed in League B, 31–13, and won more corners in 10 of 15. That was W2 D3 L2 in 2022–23 and W7 D1 L0 in 2024–25. All 15 were played at the relegated side’s own ground; Israel’s “home” is a neutral stadium in Hungary, before crowds ESPN records at 675 to 2,540 (baselines_0927.md). The structural headline: a relegated side that has been outscored 12–33 by A/B-tier opposition, but scored in all six matches it hosted in Hungary, meets a side that scores 0.90 per match at this level, whose squad voted on Friday on whether to play at all. Head to head: five meetings, the last two in 2005 World Cup qualifying (1–1, 2–2). The RESEARCH SPEC’s transfer-fee field has no national-team equivalent.
