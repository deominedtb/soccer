A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Both sides are relegated from League A. Poland were fourth in Group A1 in 2024–25 on 4 points, 9–16 (W1 D1 L4, behind Portugal, Croatia and Scotland). They had been in League A in every earlier edition, so this is their first League B campaign. Bosnia and Herzegovina were fourth in Group A3 on 2 points, 4–17 (W0 D2 L4, behind Germany, the Netherlands and Hungary). This is Bosnia’s second relegation into League B. The first time, in 2022–23, they won Group B3 (W3 D2 L1, 8–8) and went back up. In World Cup qualifying Poland were second in Group G behind the Netherlands. They beat Albania 2–1 in the play-off semi-final and lost the final 2–3 in Sweden. Bosnia were second in Group H, behind Austria. They drew both play-off ties 1–1 (Wales away, Italy at home) and won both on penalties. At the World Cup they drew with Canada (1–1), lost to Switzerland (1–4), beat Qatar (3–1) and lost to the United States (0–2) in the round of 32. The eight sides relegated from League A into League B for 2022–23 and 2024–25 took 1.91 points per home match (W12 D8 L3) and 1.43 per away match (W8 D9 L6). Their away matches had over 2.5 in 52.2% and BTTS in 60.9%, with 4.22 corners won against 4.65 conceded. No two of those eight shared a group; Group B4 is the first League B group in the three editions with two. On matchday one the eight went W3 D4 L1. Seven of them played away; the one at home, Wales, drew 0–0. Head to head: ESPN has two competitive meetings, both in 2020–21 League A Group A1. Poland won 2–1 in Zenica (7 September 2020) and 3–0 in Wrocław (14 October 2020). The previews count the full record differently (three meetings in one, five in another), and neither count is used. The sides meet again in Bosnia in this window. The RESEARCH SPEC’s transfer-fee field has no national-team equivalent. B · Style & early-season reality

Poland have two records, and the gap between them is the opposition. In League A 2024–25 under Probierz they had 40.3% of the ball, took 13.00 shots against 17.00 and won 3.67 corners against 4.17. They conceded 2.67 per match and kept no clean sheet in six. Their matches went over 2.5 in five of six. Under Urban in 25/26, against the Netherlands, Finland, Lithuania, Malta, Albania and Sweden, they had 48.2%, took 13.29 shots against 8.71, won 6.57 corners against 3.43 and scored in all seven. They still kept only one clean sheet. At home they took 17.38 shots against 10.88 and won 5.63 corners against 2.88. Urban’s side plays a back three with Cash, Zalewski and Kamiński wide and crosses 19.86 times per match. Lewandowski leads the line, with Zieliński and Szymański in midfield. The outfield spine is intact, and all nine of the most-used outfield starters are in the 23. The change is in goal: the three keepers with 13 of the 16 competitive starts are out, and Bułka’s three starts are the most among those left.

Bosnia’s record also splits by opposition. In League A 2024–25 they played a back five with 36.0% of the ball, took 7.67 shots against 18.00, won 2.00 corners against 6.00 and failed to score in half their matches. In 25/26, with a 4-4-2 and Džeko and Demirović up front, they had 47.8% and scored in 10 of 11. They also committed 15.36 fouls per match, against League B’s 11.48 per team. Away outside the World Cup they had 37.3% of the ball, took 9.57 shots against 16.43 and won 2.38 corners against 5.38. Tonight the front two, the goalkeeper and the right-back from that side are all missing. Tabaković (3 goals from 1 start) and Lukić are the likeliest options beside Demirović, and Zlomislić or Hamzić will start in goal. The predicted elevens in the previews run from a 4-3-3 to a 5-3-2. Nobody has seen tonight’s Bosnia shape in a competitive match. The evidence for it is two friendlies (0–0 with North Macedonia, 1–1 with Panama) and the Canada draw Džeko did not start.

  • The official’s record is long in Greece and short internationally. His Greek card and penalty lines are steady across two seasons. His international sample is four matches, and ESPN’s Greek box scores miss cards for most of his 2025–26 league matches.
