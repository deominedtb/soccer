D · Early-season risk notes

  • Brighton’s possession and pressing figures are so far from last season’s that they need saying plainly rather than being used quietly. Possession 70.88% against 54.07%; PPDA 4.56 against 8.88 — they win the ball back inside five opposition passes; and their opponents’ PPDA against them has risen to 24.86 from 14.60, meaning nobody is pressing them back. That is four matches, two of them against Coventry and Leeds, and it is the largest style shift anywhere on this slate. It may be a new team or it may be a fixture list.
  • The centre-back line that produced Brighton’s 46 goals conceded last season is gone in its entirety. Van Hecke (£52m), Coppola (£17m), Igor (£3m) and Webster all left; Vušković, Struijk and Svoboda arrived; Svoboda is a doubt with a knee injury. The 1.25 conceded and 1.85 xG against per match are four matches of a unit assembled in August, and the 25/26 figures beside them describe different players.
  • Arsenal are the opposite risk: their numbers are so stable that the temptation is to treat four matches as thirty-eight. The xG, xGA, PPDA and deep-completion figures do reproduce last season almost exactly, which is real evidence. But the four opponents were Coventry, Aston Villa, Chelsea and Sunderland — two of whom are 19th and 17th on this morning’s table — and Saliba is out, with White and Mosquera doubtful, so the defence that produced the 1 goal conceded may not be the defence that plays.
  • Four of Brighton’s eleven summer signings are unavailable or doubtful — Yohanna (£26m), Azeez (£15m), Hadjam (£11.1m) and Svoboda (£4.3m) — alongside Mitoma, Minteh, Ferguson, Wieffer, Hinshelwood and Tzimas. Ten names is a long list for a side whose attacking rates are the reason this card rates its scoring highly.
  • Most sensitive: the Asian handicap and total goals — both depend on which Brighton turns up, and their four margins are +4, −1, 0, +5. Least sensitive: corners and Arsenal’s side of the BTTS question — the first because both clubs’ corner rates moved less than one corner across a summer of heavy turnover, the second because Arsenal’s 27-goals-conceded season and their 0.84 xG against this season are the same finding measured twice.
