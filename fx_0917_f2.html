A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Neither club is promoted. OFI are making their debut in a European group or league phase; they qualified by winning the 2025–26 Greek Cup (3–2 after extra time against PAOK on 25 April 2026) and then beating CSKA Sofia 5–0 on aggregate in the play-off round. In the league OFI finished 6th in the regular season (10-2-14, 34–45) and were 12-3-17, 43 scored, 55 conceded, over all 32 matches including the Conference League play-off group. Hoffenheim finished 5th in the 2025–26 Bundesliga — 18-7-9, 65 scored, 52 conceded, 61 points — and qualified directly. The structural headline is that OFI’s underlying numbers were those of a side outshot most weeks (1.12 xG for, 1.64 against) that won a cup, while Hoffenheim’s summer replaced the left side and the base of midfield, and neither club has five league matches this season.

B · Style & early-season reality

OFI under Kontis were a low-possession, deep side in Greece: over his 25 league matches, 46.4% of the ball, 9.3 shots for and 16.2 against, 1.13 xG for and 1.64 against, and only 2.52 corners won against 5.56 conceded. They foul a lot in a heavy-fouling league (13.5 per match, league 14.5 per team) and were far stronger at home (7-0-5, 19–12) than away (3-3-7, 15–27). They set up 3-4-2-1 at Olympiacos. This season’s four league matches are the same shape — 2.25 corners won, 5.50 conceded, 1.10 xG against 1.31 — but have produced three clean sheets. The summer changed the striker (Kodro for Salcedo and Rakonjac) and the back three (Sielis for Lampropoulos), and Kodro’s fit is two weeks old.

Hoffenheim under Ilzer were an above-average Bundesliga side on the ball last season — 55.3% possession, 14.1 shots for and 12.2 against, 1.64 xG for and 1.50 against — and an unusually high-fouling one (13.9 per match against a league 10.4), with 2.18 cards per match. They lined up 4-1-4-1 against Stuttgart. The three league matches this season show more volume at both ends (2.13 xG for, 2.05 against, 7.33 corners won) and a pattern of leading at half-time and conceding afterwards. De Cat, Lenz, Rots, Wimmer and Daghim have at most four competitive matches together; how Touré’s output is replaced on the left is, so far, evidence from a CV and three league games.

  • Neither club has five league matches this season. OFI have four, Hoffenheim three, so every current rate on the strip is a small sample.
  • OFI’s season record mixes a cup win with a losing league campaign. Under Kontis they went 10-3-12 in the league and were outshot heavily; the cup run and this season’s clean sheets do not appear in the chance data.
  • OFI’s centre-forward is new. Kodro arrived on 29 August, after Salcedo and Rakonjac left; OFI’s attacking series belong mostly to players no longer there.
  • Most sensitive: 1X2, handicap and OFI team goals. Least sensitive: corners, where OFI’s low-won, high-conceded pattern has held under two coaches and into this season.
