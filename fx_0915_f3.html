A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Neither club is promoted. Alavés finished 14th in 2025–26 — 11 wins, 10 draws, 17 defeats, 44 scored, 56 conceded, 43 points — and reached the Copa del Rey quarter-finals, with Toni Martínez scoring 14 league goals; Eduardo Coudet left by mutual consent on 3 March 2026, in 16th, and Quique Sánchez Flores took over the same day. Valencia finished 9th — 13 wins, 10 draws, 15 defeats, 46 scored, 55 conceded, 49 points — also reaching the Copa quarter-finals, with Hugo Duro on 10. The structural headline is Valencia’s collapse and the club’s response to it: one point and one goal from five matches, and on Sunday 13 September the club dismissed Carlos Corberán — the first head coach removed in LaLiga this season — together with its football CEO Ron Gourlay and his working group. Óscar Sánchez, coach of the reserve side VCF Mestalla, takes the team as interim; tonight is his first senior match.

B · Style & early-season reality

Alavés’s shape has changed with the coach. Last season’s most-used system on Understat was a 4–4–2 (1,535 minutes), most of it Coudet’s; this season Sánchez Flores has played a back three or five in almost every minute — 3–5–2 for 278 minutes, 5–3–2 for 138 — with 44% possession, 14.0 shots for and 10.2 against per match, and the same heavy contact profile as last season (15.0 fouls and 2.60 cards per match, against 14.9 and 2.39). Corners have held at 5.40 per match, above the league’s 4.83 per team. The two new centre-backs, Valentini and Koski, both appear in probable elevens behind that system after Parada and Diarra were sold; 11 goals from 7.8 FotMob xG shows the attack finishing ahead of its chances, five of those goals in one match.

Valencia under Corberán were a 4–4–2 side last season (2,212 minutes), low-contact (12.1 fouls, 1.95 cards per match) and slow-starting (0.29 first-half goals per match). This season Corberán moved to a back five — 5–4–1 for 306 minutes — and every attacking number fell: 0.80 xG, 8.4 shots, 2.2 shots on target and 3.80 corners per match. The man who designed that shift has gone. What Óscar Sánchez will pick is unpublished beyond predicted elevens that include a player sold on 3 September, and his centre-back group is depleted by injuries to Tárrega, Diakhaby and Copete; the summer additions who could change the attacking numbers — Elliott, Sato, Arnau Martínez — are evidence by CV and a handful of matches.

D · Early-season risk notes

  • Valencia’s current numbers describe a coach and a system that may not be on the pitch. The 5–4–1 that produced 0.80 xG per match was Corberán’s; Óscar Sánchez has had two days and no senior match.
  • Valencia’s last-season rates describe a different defence. Özkaçar and Cömert have gone and Tárrega, Diakhaby and Copete are injured, so last season’s 1.45 goals conceded per match was made by centre-backs mostly unavailable tonight.
  • Alavés’s attack is running ahead of its chances. 11 goals from 7.8 FotMob xG, five of them against Osasuna; last season’s 1.16 goals per match is a 4–4–2 figure.
  • Referee sample conflict. Sesma’s LaLiga numbers this season (three matches, two reds, 5.8 fouls per card) and FotMob’s 23-match line (7.3 fouls per card) do not agree, and he has no full top-flight season.
  • Most sensitive: 1X2 — the interim appointment is the single input with no measurement. Least sensitive: Alavés’s foul and card rates, unchanged across two seasons and two coaches.
