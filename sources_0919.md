## Standing data pipeline (used on every fixture; not a search)
- ESPN public match record, per competition — `site.web.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/soccer/{slug}/teams/{id}/schedule?season={2026,2025}` for every completed match of both seasons; `/summary?event={id}` for each match's boxscore (fouls, yellow and red cards, corners, shots, shots on target, possession, penalty kicks), key events (goal minute and scoring team, for first-half goals) and `gameInfo.officials` (the match referee). `site.api.espn.com` is 403 from this machine today; `site.web.api.espn.com` serves the same data.
- ESPN season statistics objects — `sports.core.api.espn.com/v2/sports/soccer/leagues/{slug}/seasons/2026/types/1/teams/{id}/statistics/0` for expected goals and expected goals conceded. ESPN publishes no xG for 2025-26.
- ESPN standings — `site.web.api.espn.com/apis/v2/sports/soccer/{slug}/standings?season={2026,2025,2024,2023}`.
- Understat `getLeagueData/{EPL,La_liga,Serie_A,Bundesliga,Ligue_1}/{2025,2026}` for per-match xG, npxG, xGA, PPDA and deep completions, both seasons.
- **Referee records are computed, not searched.** Every completed match in a division is scanned for `gameInfo.officials`, and each official's cards per match, fouls per match, fouls per card and penalties per match are counted from the matches he actually refereed. The 2025-26 scans are complete seasons (Premier League 380, LaLiga 380, Serie A 380, Bundesliga 306, Ligue 1 305). Only the *appointment* for today costs a search.
- baselines_0919.md — league baselines. 2025-26 lines carried from baselines_0918.md; 2026-27 lines recomputed.
