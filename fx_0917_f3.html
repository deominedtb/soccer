A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Neither club is promoted. Beşiktaş finished 4th in the 2025–26 Süper Lig — 17-9-8, 59 scored, 40 conceded, 60 points — and came through three Europa League qualifying rounds. Marseille finished 5th in the 2025–26 Ligue 1 — 18-5-11, 63 scored, 45 conceded, 59 points — and qualified directly. The structural headline is that both clubs changed coach this summer and last season’s numbers belong to other men: Beşiktaş replaced Sergen Yalçın with Vincenzo Italiano and spent heavily; Marseille, on their third coach since February, replaced Habib Beye with Bruno Génésio and signed nobody.

B · Style & early-season reality

Beşiktaş under Yalçın were a possession side with a good defensive chance record for the Süper Lig — 55.5% of the ball, 16.9 shots for and 12.4 against, 1.88 xG for and 1.13 against — and a heavy-fouling one (13.6 per match). Under Italiano, in five league matches, the shape is more dominant: 61.2% possession, 8.00 corners won against 3.60, 1.81 xG for and only 0.48 against, and more fouls again (15.0). The six qualifiers produced 2.08 xG for and 0.76 against. They lined up 4-1-4-1 at the weekend. The evidence for the new midfield is real but short: Kökcü, Özcan and Ouattara have played the whole run; Vlahović joined on 12 August and Miretti on 28 August; Trossard is out.

Marseille’s last season had two halves. Under De Zerbi (21 league matches) they scored 2.19 per match with 1.84 xG and 58% possession; under Beye (12 matches) the xG held (1.82) but scoring fell to 1.25, with 0.42 first-half goals per match and a 2-0-4 away record. Under Génésio, in four matches, possession is up (60.8%) but xG is 1.50 for and 1.77 against, they have scored one first-half goal, and their fouls have dropped to 9.8 per match. The attack has lost Greenwood and Aubameyang without replacement, the defence Medina and the goalkeeper Rulli; tonight’s eleven is further unknown because Génésio has said he will rotate.

D · Early-season risk notes

  • Neither club’s 2025–26 numbers belong to tonight’s coach. Italiano has 11 matches, Génésio four; Marseille’s last season was split between De Zerbi and Beye.
  • Marseille are weaker on paper than any series on the strip. Greenwood, Aubameyang, Medina, Rulli and Timber left, nobody arrived, and Génésio has announced rotation before PSG.
  • Beşiktaş’s new side is only partly assembled. Vlahović and Miretti joined in mid- and late August, Trossard is out and Kökcü doubtful; the 0.48 xG against is five league matches.
  • Beşiktaş’s qualifying run was against modest opposition. Midtjylland, Hradec Králové and Kauno Žalgiris, with a 0–1 defeat in Kaunas.
  • Most sensitive: 1X2, handicap and Marseille team goals — all turn on an unannounced Marseille eleven. Least sensitive: discipline, where the official’s strictness holds across two published seasons.
