B · Style & early-season reality

Anderlecht last season were a contact-heavy side without control of games: 52.7% possession, 14.0 fouls per match against a Pro League mean of 11.4 per team, 2.58 cards per match, and a chance balance that ran against them (1.49 xG for, 1.62 against), which worsened in the play-offs. They took 5.73 corners per match. Under Vítor Bruno the six league matches show more of the ball (60.9%), more corners (6.83) and a much better chance balance (1.52 for, 0.90 against) — but only four goals, from 4.5 shots on target per match. What the summer changed is the spine: De Cat and Saliba out of midfield, Amané in on 2 September, Šimić sold, and now Hey and Biancone injured, so the projected centre-back pairing is Omobamidele, on loan since 2 September, with Pétrot. Evidence that this back line works together is a CV, not a match.

Lyon under Fonseca are the settled half of the fixture in shape: last season Understat logged 1,660 league minutes in a 4–2–3–1, with 54.6% possession, 12.3 shots for and 12.0 against, 13.0 fouls and 5.88 corners per match; in this competition they held 61.3% of the ball and won seven of eight league-phase matches. Their best-documented change is out wide: Fofana and Afonso Moreira, sold for a combined €68.6m, leave Nakamura (arrived 2 September) and Duranville as the new wide options alongside Nuamah, with Openda on loan at centre-forward. The four Ligue 1 matches since show fewer goals at both ends — one match over 2.5, two clean sheets — than either last season’s league or this summer’s qualifiers, where three of four went over.

D · Early-season risk notes

  • Anderlecht’s last season was coached by three different men and ended with a play-off collapse. 39 conceded in 30 regular-season matches (1.30 per match) became 23 in ten play-off matches (2.30); none of those coaches is in charge now.
  • The home back line tonight appears in none of the samples. Hey (out until January), Biancone, Sardella and Camara are injured; Omobamidele arrived on loan on 2 September.
  • Lyon’s attack was reshaped after the qualifying defeat. Fofana left on 1 September and Nakamura arrived on 2 September, so the Ligue 1 and Champions League qualifying numbers were made with different wide players.
  • Domestic and European samples point in opposite directions for both clubs on goals, and tonight’s opponent is stronger than any qualifier either faced.
  • Most sensitive: BTTS and Anderlecht team goals, which turn on an unfamiliar back line and a scoring record that contradicts its chance data. Least sensitive: corners, where both clubs sit above the baseline across two seasons.
