## Standing data pipeline (used on every fixture; not a search)
- ESPN public match record, per competition — `site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/soccer/{slug}/scoreboard?dates=20260918` for venue and kickoff; `/teams/{id}/schedule?season={2026,2025}` for every completed match of both seasons; `/summary?event={id}` for each match's boxscore (corners, fouls, yellow and red cards, shots, shots on target, possession) and key events (goal period, for first-half goals).
- ESPN season statistics objects — `sports.core.api.espn.com/v2/sports/soccer/leagues/{slug}/seasons/{2026,2025}/types/1/teams/{id}/statistics/0` for season aggregates and, for 2026-27 only, expected goals. ESPN publishes no xG for 2025-26; that gap is filled from Understat, not estimated.
- Understat `getLeagueData/{Bundesliga,Serie_A,Ligue_1,EPL,La_liga}/{2025,2026}` — per-match xG, npxG, xGA, PPDA and deep completions for every club, both seasons. Understat's league pages are now client-side; this is the endpoint the page itself calls.
- ESPN standings `site.web.api.espn.com/apis/v2/sports/soccer/{slug}/standings?season={2025,2026}` — final and current positions and points.
- ESPN cup scoreboards, scanned by date — `ger.dfb_pokal`, `ita.coppa_italia`, `eng.league_cup`, `esp.copa_del_rey`, `fra.coupe_de_france`, `uefa.champions` — for competitive matches outside the league. Copa del Rey and Coupe de France have no matches in the record before tonight; neither does the Champions League.
- baselines_0918.md — league baselines. The 2025-26 lines are carried from baselines_0917.md and cost no search; the 2026-27 lines were computed from the ESPN and Understat data above.
