Union have changed shape, and the change is measurable rather than asserted. The early cost is visible in the same rows: xG against has risen from 1.65 to 2.91 per match, deep completions conceded from 7.03 to 11.67, and shots faced from 12.9 to 17.3, with ten goals conceded in three. The new centre-back line is the unit being asked to hold that higher line, and it is four players who were not at the club in May, bought for €3m between them, two of whom one preview says are unavailable tonight. The evidence that this is a system change is a coach’s start date and three matches of pressing data, not a season — and the away fixture that produced the clearest reading of it, 0–4 at Leverkusen, is also the least representative sample a new back line could give.
