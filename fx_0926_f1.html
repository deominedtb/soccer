A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Slovenia stay in League B: third in Group B3 in 2024–25 on 8 points, 7–9 (W2 D2 L2, behind Norway and Austria). They then kept their place in the B/C play-off against Slovakia, 0–0 in Bratislava and 1–0 after extra time in Ljubljana (0–0 at 90 minutes). Scotland are relegated from League A: third in Group A1 on 7 points, 7–8 (W2 D1 L3, behind Portugal and Croatia). They then lost the A/B play-off to Greece, 1–0 in Piraeus and 0–3 at Hampden. In World Cup qualifying Slovenia were third in Group B on 4 points, 3–8, without a win (D4 L2, behind Switzerland and Kosovo). Scotland won Group C on 13 points, 13–7 (W4 D1 L1, ahead of Denmark), then went out of the World Cup in the group stage, third in Group C on 3 points, 1–4 (Haiti 1–0, Morocco 0–1, Brazil 0–3). The structural headline is two coaches in their first competitive match, each without his predecessor’s top scorer: Šeško for Slovenia, McTominay for Scotland. The eight sides relegated from League A into League B for 2022–23 and 2024–25 took 1.43 points per away match over 23 (W8 D9 L6), scoring 1.52 and conceding 1.35, with over 2.5 in 52.2% and BTTS in 60.9%; four of the eight won their League B group. All eight went down as fourth-placed League A sides; Scotland came down through the play-off, a route none of the eight took. On matchday one seven of the eight were away and went W3 D3 L1, and two of those seven played in Ljubljana (Slovenia 0–2 Sweden, June 2022; Slovenia 1–1 Austria, September 2024). Scotland’s last League B edition, 2022–23, ended with them winning Group B1 on 13 points (W4 D1 L1, 11–5). Slovenia’s League B home record over the last two editions is W2 D2 L2, 9–10. Head to head: five meetings, Scotland W2 D3 (ESPN, Scottish FA). The last two were 2018 World Cup qualifiers: 1–0 to Scotland at Hampden and 2–2 in Ljubljana, where Cesar was sent off in his 100th international (Planet Nogomet). The sides meet again at Hampden on 6 October. The RESEARCH SPEC’s transfer-fee field has no national-team equivalent.
