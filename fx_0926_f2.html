A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

San Marino are promoted from League D: first in Group D1 in 2024–25 on 7 points, 5–3 (W2 D1 L1, ahead of Gibraltar and Liechtenstein), directly, as a group winner (ESPN standings; Wikipedia’s 2026–27 page). The two wins, 1–0 at home and 3–1 in Vaduz, both against Liechtenstein, were the first two competitive wins in San Marino’s history (Sports Mole). In World Cup qualifying they finished fifth and last in Group H on 0 points, 2–39 (L8, behind Austria, Bosnia-Herzegovina, Romania and Cyprus). Finland are relegated from League B: fourth in Group B2 on 0 points, 2–13 (L6, behind England, Greece and the Republic of Ireland), directly. The structural headline is the two-division gap: a side up from League D meeting a side down from League B, with no League C evidence on either side but San Marino’s two qualifiers against Cyprus. The four sides promoted from League D into League C in 2022–23 and 2024–25 (Faroe Islands, Gibraltar, Estonia, Latvia) took 0.71 points per match over 24 (W4 D5 L15), scoring 0.71 and conceding 2.00; at home 1.08 points per match (W4 D1 L7), 0.92 scored and 1.50 conceded; on matchday one they lost all four, 1–13 on aggregate; none finished above third.
