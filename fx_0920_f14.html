B · Style & early-season reality

Juventus have taken an already controlling side and pushed it to the edge of the dataset. PPDA has fallen from 10.58 to 8.19 — a 23% increase in pressing intensity — on possession barely moved at 57.1% against 57.5%, with shots up from 16.82 to 18.50. The territorial consequence is the most extreme single figure on the board: 9.75 corners won per match against 2.25 conceded, a +7.50 differential, having been +1.71 last season. They took 12 corners at Frosinone, 10 at home to Parma, 7 against Milan and 10 at Sassuolo. The defensive model is correspondingly severe: 0.40 xG conceded per match, the best figure of any club anywhere on this board, against 0.95 last season — though the counted rate has gone the other way, 0.89 to 1.00, because three of the four goals they have conceded came in one match at Sassuolo. The cost is in fouls: 16.00 per match against 12.13 last season and a divisional 11.21, the highest figure on this board — which is what a press at 8.19 PPDA produces. And the scoring has gone quiet at the start of matches: 0.25 first-half goals against 0.63, with none conceded before the break at all.

Atalanta under Sarri have moved in exactly the opposite direction on every one of those measures. PPDA has risen from 10.63 to 21.55 — the most passive figure of any club on this board bar Schalke, and a doubling of the number of opposition passes allowed per defensive action — while possession has fallen from 55.2% to 48.1% and deep completions from 9.34 to 6.00. The attacking model has more than halved: 0.84 xG per match against 1.81 last season, the fourth-lowest on this board, on shots down from 14.79 to 12.00. The defensive model has gone the same way: 2.20 xG conceded against 1.36. The corner column matches: 2.25 won against 5.68 last season and 5.50 conceded against 4.21. Their card rate is the third-lowest on this board at 0.75 per match, on 9.50 fouls — a side that does not press does not foul — and against Roma they were out-shot 39–6. Two clubs that finished 6th and 7th with almost identical records have, in one summer, arrived at opposite ends of every territorial series in this dataset.
