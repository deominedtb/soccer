A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Neither club is promoted. Sturm Graz topped the 2025–26 Austrian Bundesliga regular season (12 wins, 2 draws, 8 defeats, 33–26) and finished 2nd after the championship round — 16 wins, 8 draws, 8 defeats, 51 scored, 35 conceded over 32 matches. In last season’s Europa League league phase they finished 26th, scoring 5 and conceding 11 in eight matches. This summer they beat Hearts 6–0 on aggregate and lost 3–0 on aggregate to Fenerbahçe in Champions League qualifying. Rennes finished 6th in 2025–26 Ligue 1 — 59 points, 59 scored, 50 conceded — and took a Europa League place when Lens won the Coupe de France. The structural headline is the gap between Sturm’s domestic and European evidence, set against a Rennes back line that lost three centre-backs this summer.

B · Style & early-season reality

Sturm at home in Austria are a front-foot, corner-heavy side: 54.0% possession, 1.67 xG for against 1.30 last season, 5.56 corners per match rising to 7.33 this season, 11.6 fouls and 2.34 cards per match. Their European record is a different team: in last season’s league phase they averaged 45.0% of the ball, 0.73 xG for and 1.75 against, 2.75 corners and 17.1 fouls per match, and this summer Fenerbahçe held them scoreless over two legs. Under Ingolitsch the domestic chance creation has risen (2.10 xG per league match) without the goals following (1.33). What the summer changed is the centre-back pairing — Mitchell sold on 19 August, Quarshie in on the 27th — and the forward line, where Grgić is injured and Diakité arrived two weeks ago.

Rennes under Haise are the more open side in the numbers: 71% of last season’s league matches went over 2.5, they conceded 1.47 per match with 1.56 xG against, and this season’s four matches show 2.05 xG against per match with 49.9% of the ball — less than last season’s 53.5%. They took 6.00 corners per match last season but 4.25 this season. The defensive change is large and late: Jacquet, Brassier and Østigård have all gone, Brassier on deadline day, while Cresswell arrived on 4 August and Oliveira in June. Dia arrived on 1 September; evidence that he fits the attack is a CV, not a match.

D · Early-season risk notes

  • Sturm’s domestic numbers do not transfer to Europe on the evidence. 26th last season with 0.73 xG for and 1.75 against per match, and no goal in 180 minutes against Fenerbahçe.
  • Rennes’ defence is new. Jacquet, Brassier and Østigård have gone; the four league matches since produced 2.05 xG against per match.
  • Sturm’s coach changed at mid-season. Last season’s figures mix Säumel’s half with Ingolitsch’s.
  • Team news is reported inconsistently. Previews disagree on Weiper and Diakité, and the only Rennes probable eleven reached contains a player no longer at the club.
  • Most sensitive: 1X2, handicap and Sturm team goals — each turns on whether Sturm’s domestic or European series applies. Least sensitive: BTTS, where both clubs sit above the baseline in both seasons and have scored in every league match this season.
