A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Neither club is promoted, and both are reigning champions. Hapoel Be’er Sheva won the 2025–26 Israeli Premier League — 24 wins, 7 draws, 5 defeats, 79 scored, 38 conceded over 36 matches including the championship round — and reached the Champions League play-off round, where Sabah beat them 6–4 on aggregate after extra time. Dinamo Zagreb won the 2025–26 Croatian league — 27 wins, 5 draws, 4 defeats, 93 scored, 28 conceded over 36 matches — and also went out in the play-off round, 5–3 on aggregate to Viking. Division note: neither domestic league publishes xG through any source reached, and the Israeli league’s yellow-card totals are not published either; the xG on this card comes from UEFA qualifiers and from Dinamo’s 2025–26 Europa League campaign. The structural headline is the venue: because of the Gaza war, Israeli clubs must play UEFA home matches at neutral grounds, so this Be’er Sheva “home” match is in Bucharest, and the home record in the strip was made at Turner Stadium in the league.

B · Style & early-season reality

Be’er Sheva were a possession side at home last season: 61.0% of the ball, 7.0 shots on target and 5.97 corners per league match, with 13.8 fouls per match against an Israeli mean of 13.4 per team. The summer qualifiers show a different team once opposition quality rose: 50.8% possession, 9.7 shots for against 13.5, 3.67 corners for against 5.83, and in the second leg at Sabah 35 shots and 4.23 xG against with two players sent off. What the summer changed is the forward line — three new centre-forwards — and the evidence that it works is league goals against Israeli opposition; tonight the midfield and centre-back losses (Peretz, Ventura, Diop, Rotman) matter more to the shape than any arrival.

Dinamo under Kovačević dominate domestically — 57.6% possession, 6.28 corners and 11.9 fouls per league match last season, rising to 64.1%, 7.83 and 15.2 this season — and averaged 60.3% possession and 19.3 shots for against 9.7 over six qualifiers, with 2.27 xG for and 1.22 against in the four with full data. The Europa League last season showed the other side: 49.9% possession, 1.31 xG for and 1.43 against, 22 goals conceded in ten matches and a knockout-play-off exit to Genk after extra time. The structural change is in goal: Kotarski arrived on 31 August, after all six qualifiers had been played, and Dinamo conceded four at Osijek on Saturday.

D · Early-season risk notes

  • The venue is neutral. Be’er Sheva’s 42–19 league home record was made at Turner Stadium; tonight is in Bucharest.
  • Be’er Sheva’s defence has been conceding heavily against better opposition. 18 goals in five defeats this season, including 2–5 at Sabah and 1–4 v Maccabi Tel Aviv, and tonight without Diop and Rotman at centre-back.
  • Dinamo’s domestic numbers overstate their European ones. +65 goal difference in the league last season against −6 in ten Europa League matches; the goalkeeper is new.
  • No xG exists for either club in either league. Every chance-based statement on this card rests on UEFA matches, some of them only two or four long.
  • Most sensitive: 1X2, handicap and discipline — neutral venue, missing chance data and a conflicting referee record. Least sensitive: corners and BTTS, where both clubs’ series are published and sit on the same side of the baseline.
