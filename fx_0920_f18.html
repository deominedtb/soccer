B · Style & early-season reality

PSG are the same side territorially and a different one in front of goal, and the gap between those two statements is the most interesting thing on this card. Possession 67.5% against 68.8%, deep completions 11.75 against 11.79, shots 18.75 against 17.71 — three series essentially unchanged, with PPDA loosening modestly from 7.96 to 9.35. And yet xG has fallen from 2.23 to 1.57 per match, a drop of two-thirds of a goal, on the same shot count — the same volume producing materially worse chances, which is what selling three centre-forwards and buying three wide forwards looks like in the data. Goals have followed: 1.50 per match against 2.18, with a failed-to-score rate that remains 0.0%. The defensive side has not moved at all: 0.98 xG conceded against 0.99, among the two or three best figures on this board in both seasons — but the counted concession has risen from 0.85 to 1.50, and the clean-sheet rate has collapsed from 52.9% to 25.0%. The corner column is the most extreme number in this entire dataset: 0.75 conceded per match, from four matches reading 0, 0, 0 and 3. They foul 7.25 times a match, the second-lowest figure on this board, and did so last season too at 9.18.

Marseille have lost €127m of players and their numbers have fallen roughly in proportion. xG has dropped from 2.04 to 1.66 and non-penalty xG to 1.47, shots from 15.09 to 13.50, while xG conceded has risen from 1.46 to 1.87 — both ends worse, with possession actually up from 56.9% to 60.8% and PPDA barely moved (10.04 to 10.70). More territory, worse chances at both ends: the signature of a squad that has sold its output and kept its shape. The venue split that defined last season has sharpened into something absolute: six goals in two home matches (4–0 and 2–3) and none at all in two away, plus a 4–1 defeat in Istanbul on Thursday. Their 50.0% failed-to-score rate is entirely an away phenomenon. The discipline column has fallen sharply: 9.75 fouls and 1.25 cards per match against 12.71 and 2.44 last season — a side that was one of the division’s most-booked is now among its quietest, on four matches and with no stated mechanism. Both clubs’ first-half figures have moved the same way and the same amount: scoring roughly halved, concession roughly doubled.

The shot count and deep completions are unchanged and the xG has fallen from 2.23 to 1.57 — the clearest evidence on this slate that volume and chance quality are different things.
  • Marseille’s incoming window could not be established at all. €112.5m of confirmed departures against exactly one confirmed arrival in any source reached. Whether the club signed nobody or whether nothing reached here recorded it is not determined, and the probable eleven contains at least one player the same source says left in July.
  • Marseille played in Istanbul on Thursday and lost 4–1. That is three days before this fixture, and it is the most recent evidence about them; it sits outside the league sample every rate on this card is built from.
  • Both clubs’ discipline has halved with no mechanism. Marseille 12.71 fouls to 9.75 and 2.44 cards to 1.25; PSG 9.18 to 7.25. The division has fallen too, 3.91 cards to 3.21, and the appointed official’s own two samples disagree by more than two cards a match. Four separate low readings converging is a stack, not a finding.
  • Most sensitive: 1X2, Asian handicap and the halves markets — two clubs with identical four-match goal records, a division where home advantage has fallen to 38.1%, and four first-half series all in motion. Least sensitive: Corners — a 0.75-per-match concession continuous with 34 prior matches, and possession, deep-completion and foul data to explain it.
