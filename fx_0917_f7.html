A · Squad, promotion & transfer context

Lillestrøm are a promoted side: they won the 2025 OBOS-ligaen unbeaten — 25-5-0, 87 scored, 18 conceded, 80 points — and the 2025 Norwegian Cup as a second-tier club, 3–1 against Sarpsborg 08 on 6 December 2025, which put them into the Europa League play-off round, where they beat Egnatia. Torreense are not promoted and still play in Liga Portugal 2: 3rd there in 2025–26 (18-5-11, 46–33), they won the Taça de Portugal 2–1 after extra time against Sporting CP on 24 May 2026 and entered the league phase directly. Both clubs are making their debut in a European group or league phase; Torreense are playing their first European match of any kind. The structural headline is that neither club’s last season was played in a top division, and only Lillestrøm have a published top-flight series since.

B · Style & early-season reality

Lillestrøm under Ødegaard dominated the second tier in 2025 — 57% possession, 14.7 shots for and 7.8 against, 6.07 corners won, 1.40 first-half goals per match — and have been an even, open top-flight side since: 51.4% possession, 1.65 xG for and 1.57 against, 5.20 corners won against 5.60 conceded, and a low card rate (1.70 per match in a 3.49-card league). They play 4-1-4-1. The home record is split (5-0-5), and the defeats include 0–4 at Bodø/Glimt and 1–4 at home to Fredrikstad. The summer took Karlsbakk and Drammeh out of the midfield and wide areas; Bassi and Ottosson arrived at the end of August, and Gulbrandsen, who has Europa League experience with Molde, Salzburg and Başakşehir, leads the line.

Torreense’s evidence is thinner by construction. Under Tralhão in the second half of last season’s Liga Portugal 2 they became a tight side — 12-3-3, 13 conceded in 18, 2.83 shots on target against per match, 50% clean sheets — winning fewer corners (3.67) than they conceded (4.61). This season’s six league matches have gone the other way (nine conceded, one win, 0.83 first-half goals conceded), and Liga Portugal 2 publishes nothing beyond goals for them. The summer brought three new centre-forwards and several loans, the last on 2–5 September; how that side fits is a CV and six matches with no statistics attached.

D · Early-season risk notes

  • Both clubs’ last seasons were second-tier seasons. Lillestrøm’s 87–18 in the OBOS-ligaen fell to 28–26 in Eliteserien; Torreense are still in Liga Portugal 2.
  • Torreense’s current form is unmeasured beyond goals. Six league matches, one win, nine conceded, and no published shots, xG, corners, cards or fouls.
  • Torreense’s bench arrangement is unusual. Tralhão does not hold the UEFA Pro licence and his assistant fronted the pre-match press conference; how the bench is registered for UEFA matches was not reached.
  • Lillestrøm sold their cup-final match-winner. Karlsbakk and Drammeh left in July and August; Bassi and Ottosson arrived at the end of August.
  • Most sensitive: 1X2, handicap, Torreense team goals, discipline and corners — each needs a Torreense statistic that is not published. Least sensitive: BTTS and totals, where both sides’ goal series are complete, if at different levels.
